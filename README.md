# Savory-Bites-Sales-Performance-Analysis-Excel-Based-Data-Exploration-Dashboard
A data analysis project focused on uncovering key business insights from a fictional restaurant’s sales data using Microsoft Excel. The analysis identifies best-selling products, top-performing managers, preferred payment methods, and regional performance to support data-driven business decisions and improve overall restaurant efficiency.

## Project Overview
Savory Bites is a fictional restaurant that sells a variety of food items such as Burgers, Fries, Chicken Sandwiches, Beverages, and others.  
This project analyzes the restaurant’s sales data to uncover key insights including best-selling products, top-performing managers, preferred payment methods, and city-level performance.

The goal is to provide actionable insights that can help management optimize operations, improve customer experience, and increase overall revenue.

---

## Objectives
The main objectives of this analysis are to:
- Identify the **best-selling food items**.
- Understand **sales trends** across time periods.
- Highlight the **most used payment methods**.
- Determine the **best-performing managers**.
- Analyze the **preferred purchase types**.
- Discover the **top-performing cities**.

---

## Dataset Overview
**Source:** Kaggle  
**Tool Used:** Microsoft Excel  

The dataset contains sales information from **November to December 2022**, including the following fields:
- **Order ID:** Unique identifier for each order  
- **Date:** Date of purchase  
- **Product:** Name of the food product  
- **Price:** Product price in USD  
- **Quantity:** Units sold  
- **Purchase Type:** Online, In-store, or Drive-thru  
- **Payment Method:** Cash, Credit Card, or Gift Card  
- **Manager:** Sales manager responsible for each sale  
- **City:** City where each transaction occurred  

---

## Data Preparation
The dataset was prepared and cleaned in Excel using the following steps:
1. **Removed duplicates** and checked for missing values.  
2. **Standardized data types** for consistency.  
3. **Created calculated fields** (e.g., Total Price = Price × Quantity).  
4. Organized data into pivot tables for easy aggregation and exploration.

---

## Analysis and Visualizations
Key charts and visualizations created include:
- **Product by Amount** – Identified Burgers as the top-selling item.  
- **Performance by Manager** – Joao Silva ranked as the best-performing manager.  
- **Payment Method by Amount** – Credit Cards were the most used method.  
- **Performance by City** – Lisbon emerged as the top-performing city.  
- **Monthly and Weekly Trends** – December was the peak month; Wednesday recorded the highest sales.  
- **Purchase Type by Amount** – Online purchases accounted for 39.66% of total revenue.

All findings were combined into a **comprehensive interactive dashboard** built in Excel.

## Key Insights
- **Top Manager:** Joao Silva generated the highest revenue.  
- **Best-Selling Item:** Burgers with total sales of \$376,999.81.  
- **Most Used Payment Method:** Credit Card with \$361,715.03 in transactions.  
- **Top City:** Lisbon led all cities in revenue.  
- **Peak Month:** December (56.84% of total revenue).  
- **Peak Day:** Wednesday showed the highest sales volume.  

---

## Recommendations
- **Leverage Peak Days:** Introduce Wednesday specials or loyalty points to maintain momentum.  
- **Run Monthly Campaigns:** Replicate December’s success across other months with targeted promotions.  
- **Boost Drive-Thru Sales:** Launch “Express Drive-Thru” offers or discounts to encourage usage.  
- **Encourage Alternative Payments:** Offer rewards for using credit or gift cards.  
- **Promote Underperforming Products:** Use digital campaigns and limited-time discounts.  
- **Recognize Top Performers:** Reward and reassign high-performing managers to underperforming cities.
